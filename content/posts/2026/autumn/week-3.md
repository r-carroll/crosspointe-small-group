## Scripture
[Romans 16:25-27](https://www.bible.com/bible/59/ROM.16.ESV)

## Questions 
1. After sixteen chapters explaining the gospel, Paul ends Romans with praise.
What does that teach us about the relationship between understanding
biblical truth and worshiping God?

2. Paul describes God as the One who is able to strengthen His people. Why is it
important to remember that our spiritual stability ultimately depends upon
God’s power rather than our own strength?

3. Paul says the gospel that was once hidden has now been revealed and made
known to all nations to bring about the “obedience of faith.
” What does this
tell us about God’s purpose in revealing the gospel?

4. Paul calls God “the only wise God.” After everything Paul has explained in
Romans, what should God’s incomparable wisdom produce in us?

## Next Steps 
Identify one truth about God or the gospel
from our study of Romans that has particularly
changed you, and intentionally turn that truth
into praise.

## Prayer Focus
Ask Him to deepen your dependence upon Him
and make your life an increasingly visible
declaration of Paul’s final words: “To the only
wise God be glory forevermore through Jesus
Christ! Amen.”
