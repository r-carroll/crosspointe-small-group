## Scripture
[Romans 11:13-16](https://www.bible.com/bible/1/ROM.14.KJV)

## Questions 
1. Throughout this passage, Paul repeatedly places others' spiritual well-being above
personal freedom. Why does he consider love a higher priority than exercising
Christian liberty?
2. Paul warns believers not to place a stumbling block before another Christian. What
does it mean to protect another believer's spiritual well-being, and why should that
concern outweigh personal preference?
3. Paul says the kingdom of God is about righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy
Spirit. How does remembering God's priorities reshape our understanding of
Christian liberty?
4. Paul concludes that whatever does not proceed from faith is sin. Why is acting
according to a clear conscience

## Next Steps 
This week, before making a decision in an
area of Christian liberty, ask two questions:
Will this strengthen another believer? and
Can I do this with a clear conscience before
God?

## Prayer Focus
Pray that God would produce a church where
love consistently governs liberty. Ask Him to
help you protect the consciences of fellow
believers, pursue what strengthens the body of
Christ, and live each day with a conscience
shaped by His Word and confident before Him.
